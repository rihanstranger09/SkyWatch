Satellite & drone imagery,
processed on drop.

A GeoTIFF lands in S3 and the platform does the rest: an SQS-buffered, containerised Lambda computes NDVI & NDWI with rasterio/GDAL, writes a Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF plus a preview, and records bounds, CRS and index statistics in DynamoDB. GitHub Actions builds the image, deploys the SAM stack and then proves the whole path works with a synthetic raster.

Architecture

The data path, end to end

Serverless, buffered and observable: S3 notifications feed SQS, the queue drives a container-image Lambda, and failures retry into a dead-letter queue with an alarm on top.

make e2e              # upload a synthetic raster + verify
